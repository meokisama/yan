# Card

A puffy card on a candy edge, with a wash of its colour across the top. Make
it `interactive` and it leans toward the pointer, catches a glare, grows a
star on its corner and wobbles like jelly when you click it. ぷるん

## Examples

### Interactive

`interactive` turns the whole card into one target. Move the pointer over it
and it leans after you with a glare; click and it squashes, throws emphasis
lines and dust, and bursts. Pair it with `asChild` and a link or a button so
it can be focused. Each card here takes a different recipe from [Effects](/docs/effects).

<ComponentPreview name="card-interactive" />

```tsx
<Card interactive asChild fx={fxRecipes.petals}>
  <Link href="/sakura">…</Link>
</Card>
```

## Accessibility

- A plain card is a `<div>` with no role. Give it a heading inside.
- An interactive card needs `asChild` with a link or a button: a bare `<div>` cannot be reached by keyboard.
- The lean and the glare follow a mouse or a pen, not a finger.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the lean, the wobble and the particles are skipped.
