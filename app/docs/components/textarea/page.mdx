# Textarea

A soft, roomy field that grows with its text, and a mochi who keeps you
company. She dozes behind the top edge, pops up with a "！" when you focus the
field, follows the caret with her eyes and beams while you type. Every
keystroke kicks a sparkle off the caret, a new line drops a heart, and
deleting leaves a little puff.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Textarea } from "@/components/ui/textarea"
```

```tsx
<Textarea placeholder="Tell us everything" />
```

For other bursts, pass a recipe from [Effects](/docs/effects):
`<Textarea fx={{ ...fxRecipes.hearts, count: 2 }} />`. Keep `count` low: it fires on every keystroke.

In kawaii mode the field sits in a full-width wrapper that holds the mochi, and
`className` still goes to the field. The mochi stands about 28px above the top
edge, so leave that much room over the field.

## Accessibility

- It renders a native `<textarea>`. Name it with a `Label`.
- Text is 16px below the `sm` breakpoint, so iOS does not zoom the page on focus.
- Growing with the text uses `field-sizing: content`. Browsers without it show a fixed-height field that scrolls.
- The mochi is decoration: hidden from screen readers and out of the pointer's way.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, she pops up without the bounce, and no particles are drawn.
